# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Allan Grønlund and Kasper Green Larsen for making this work
available and for developing a precise separation result for quantum and
quantum-inspired classical algorithms for linear systems.

The paper connects a concrete random-walk oracle game, sparse linear-system
structure, and quantum-inspired sampling lower bounds. This repository is an
independent, respectful audit: it records the finite mechanism that was
actually checked and keeps the asymptotic lower bound, quantum upper bound,
and reduction proof outside the evidence boundary.
